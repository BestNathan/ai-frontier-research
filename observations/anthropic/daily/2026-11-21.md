## Engineering question: how can a multi-agent research platform turn candidate scientific results into auditable evidence?

The infrastructure problem is **not** whether a model can produce an exciting answer. It is how to preserve the identity and lineage of a hypothesis, derivation, independent cross-check, formal statement, human review, and final publication without silently conflating agreement with correctness.

Proposed invariants (research synthesis, not a disclosed Anthropic implementation):

1. Every hypothesis, experiment, proof statement, manuscript version and verifier run has an immutable identity and parent evidence references.
2. Parallel agents should independently compute key results before reading one another's answers when the goal is independent verification.
3. A numerical match, a symbolic derivation, a machine-checked proof, and a human subject-matter review are different evidence types, with different guarantees.
4. Negative experiments and abandoned hypotheses remain queryable; successful runs cannot erase the search denominator.
5. Final claims must be scoped to exactly the mathematical assumptions and proof artifacts they establish.

## 1. Research-fleet scheduling and verification independence

### Verified — source-supported, author-reported

- Anthropic says a physicist asked Claude to identify theoretical-physics open problems. It proposed **22 candidates**, which were assigned to separate research agents able to read one another's progress; the agents ran in the background for approximately **six weeks**. This is a case study, not a benchmark of autonomous discovery rate.
- For the Hawking-Page calculation, Anthropic reports that almost every part was independently computed twice and checked against previously known special cases. A separate Claude-assisted calculation using another method reportedly reached the same conclusion.
- For the modular-bootstrap problem, one agent identified a numerical pattern; another, working on a sphere-packing-related problem, supplied a transferable mathematical method. An earlier agent had reportedly failed in **61 parallel attempts**. Other agents later independently obtained the same result.
- The public article says a portion of the modular-bootstrap proof was formally checked in **Lean in September**, and a physicist checked the main results. This does **not** establish that every statement in both manuscripts has a public, reproducible, end-to-end Lean certificate.
- The manuscripts explicitly disclose substantial AI generation and human review. The first reports cross-checks against known special cases; the second reports a separate publicly available model independently recovering the main result.

### Inferred — workflow abstraction

This is a heterogeneous **hypothesis DAG** rather than a single linear chat: candidate generation -> literature/numerical exploration -> independent derivations -> cross-domain transfer -> symbolic/formal verification -> subject-matter review -> manuscript. Its critical engineering property is evidence provenance across independent agents and artifact types. Collaboration accelerates search but can compromise independence if candidate solutions are shared before checks.

### Unknown

Exact scheduler, tool/runtime versions, context budgets, per-agent compute, failure/restart semantics, checkpoint format, agent prompts, selection criteria for the 22 problems, full 61-attempt denominator, independence controls, complete Lean artifacts and total cost. The articles do not provide enough data to estimate discovery probability or throughput per GPU-dollar.

## 2. Hawking-Page transition: numerical and symbolic cross-checks

### Verified — manuscript and article claims, not independent reproduction

- The manuscript studies weakly coupled `N=4` super Yang-Mills theory on `S^3`, testing whether the deconfinement transition remains first order. It identifies the sign of a three-loop quartic Polyakov-loop effective-action coefficient as decisive.
- The authors report `b = -1.48e-3 * lambda^2`, yielding a first-order transition; their Hamiltonian computation reproduces known two-loop and subsector results and independently checks nearly every number.
- This is a specific mathematical-model result. It does not establish an observed black-hole phase transition in our universe or solve quantum gravity generally.

### Inferred

For lengthy symbolic/numerical derivations, verification should retain intermediate expression DAGs, independent implementations of critical subcomputations, regularization conventions, precision/rounding settings, and references to limiting cases. Merely matching a final scalar is insufficient when two solvers may share an upstream mistaken assumption.

### Unknown

Complete computational environment and reproducibility of every intermediate diagram from the currently inspected abstract; independent peer review and broader physical applicability.

## 3. Modular bootstrap: search-to-conjecture-to-proof

### Verified — manuscript and article claims, not independent reproduction

- The manuscript studies the spinless modular bootstrap for two-dimensional conformal field theories under stated conditions, including unitary compact theories with `c > 1` and no conserved currents beyond Virasoro.
- It reports an exact large-`c` coefficient `Lambda_V = 12*pi^2 / (pi^2 + 4*asinh(1)^2) ~= 9.1266`, with upper and lower bounds in a specified class of functionals.
- The authors describe an electrostatic-potential/spectral-curve method for establishing the optimal slope, and separately identify a **conjectural** subleading correction supported by numerical experiments up to `c = 2048`.
- Anthropic says numerical exploration suggested a pattern, followed by a new analytic method, additional agent derivations, Lean checking and human review. The result is scoped to this version of the bootstrap, not a complete solution of 3D quantum gravity.

### Inferred

An effective scientific-agent platform should label each artifact as **observed numerical regularity**, **conjecture**, **analytic derivation**, **machine-checked theorem**, or **expert-reviewed statement**, and enforce transitions only with the relevant evidence. Formal verification proves a formal statement under its axioms and trusted kernel, not the validity of the physical model or every informal interpretation.

### Unknown

The full public verifier source revision and dependency closure, whether the paper's entire argument is formally represented, numerical seed/precision, completeness of independent-agent cross-checks and external replication status.

## Competing architectural options

| Approach | Advantage | Failure / cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One long-lived research agent and shared notes | Low coordination overhead | Single reasoning failure contaminates all checks; context drift |
| Independent agents with sealed inputs, then compare | Better independence and discrepancy detection | Duplicate compute, high cost; agreement can still share training priors |
| Shared task DAG and evidence graph | Explicit dependencies, partial retries, audit trail | Scheduler, leases, stale claims, storage and merge contention |
| Formal checker plus artifact-pinned statements | Strong correctness guarantee for the *formalized* proposition | Formalization gap, trusted kernel/axioms, substantial authoring overhead |
| Human expert review plus numerical regression | Domain interpretation and external sanity checks | Expert time, selection bias, weaker formal guarantees |

A hybrid system can combine independent derivations with an evidence graph, selected formal proofs and human review; it must not treat their assurances as interchangeable.

## Proposed falsifiable experiments (not yet executed)

**Experiment A — Independent scientific cross-checking.** Select 20 public, reproducible mathematical derivations with known ground truth and planted subtle errors. Pin the task dataset, model/API version, prompts, toolchain and dependency digests, resource budget and checker SHA. Randomize across (1) single agent self-review, (2) two agents sharing all notes, (3) two agents blinded until result submission, (4) blinded agents plus independent symbolic/formal verifier. Measure incorrect-result acceptance, false rejection, time to validated answer, token/GPU cost, human reviewer minutes and error-correlation conditional on shared context. Repeat across seeds; report confidence intervals and all failed attempts.

**Experiment B — Crash-safe hypothesis DAG.** Run 10/100-agent candidate searches with forced worker restarts, delayed messages, duplicated claims, poisoned intermediate notes and contradictory numerical results. Require exactly-once publication identities but idempotent execution, immutable artifact hashes, parent-edge provenance, lease expiry and independent evidence status. Measure recovery completeness, stale-evidence acceptance, p95 critical-path latency, conflicts, cost and expert interventions. Compare an append-only event log with a transactional SQL task graph and plain shared Markdown notes.

**Experiment C — Formalization gap.** Pin an available Lean proof artifact and its exact dependencies (if the authors publish them), independently build it, enumerate axioms/admitted lemmas, match theorem statements to paper claims, and test small deliberate changes that should fail. Without accessible artifacts, record the gap as **Unknown** rather than claiming independent formal verification.

## Conclusion and limitations

**Verified (source-supported):** Anthropic describes a six-week, multi-agent research effort producing two theoretical-physics manuscripts; primary manuscript abstracts state specific scoped mathematical results and disclose AI generation plus human checks.  
**Inferred:** The transferable systems pattern is a provenance-aware, independently verified scientific hypothesis DAG with distinct numerical, formal and human evidence.  
**Hypothesis:** Blinded independent derivations plus artifact-pinned formal checks reduce false acceptance more than unblinded self-review at an acceptable additional cost.  
**Unknown:** Independent correctness of the published physics results, complete reproducibility of all calculations, and total research-fleet cost. None were established by this intake.

This is an AI-for-science **research-methodology** observation, not a claim that Anthropic has publicly specified a general scientific-agent runtime or that its physics results have been independently replicated.
